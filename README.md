# vrinda sales dashboard
Project Overview

This project analyzes Vrinda Store's sales data using Excel to understand sales performance, customer behavior, product performance, and other business trends.

The main goal of the project was to transform raw sales data into meaningful insights and answer important business questions through a simple and interactive dashboard.

Objectives
Analyze overall sales performance
Understand customer purchasing patterns
Identify top-performing products and categories
Analyze sales by different customer segments
Identify important sales trends
Answer key business questions using data
Present insights through an interactive Excel dashboard

Key Business Questions

The analysis focused on questions such as:

What is the overall sales performance?
Which categories generate the most sales?
Which products perform the best?
Who are the major customer segments?
Which locations contribute the most to sales?
What purchasing patterns can be identified?
Which areas of the business require more attention?
Dashboard

The final Excel dashboard provides a simple visual overview of the sales data and allows business performance to be understood through charts, KPIs, and summarized metrics.

Outcome

The project helped me practice converting raw sales data into actionable business insights.

More importantly, it helped me understand that Data Analytics is not just about creating charts. The real objective is to use data to answer business questions and support better decision-making.
